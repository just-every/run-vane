# Local agent work

The owner can request reviewable local work through the authenticated Vane HTTP API. These routes require the current native owner session and same-origin POST requests. They add no outbound grant and make no provider claim. The existing instrumentation GitHub connection remains limited to its approved exact script diff.

- `POST /api/vane/agent/draft` accepts `siteId`, ended evidence dates `from` and `to`, `title`, `effect`, `guardrail`, `measurement` and `target`. Each text field is plain text of at most 280 characters. Destinations are github, pane, webflow, mango and ads; all are draft-only. The draft records the expected effect, guardrail and measurement plan.
- `POST /api/vane/agent/ship` refuses every draft destination. A person's approval does not invent a grant. Pane edits, general copy PRs, redirects, campaign writes and budget writes remain unavailable.
- `GET /api/vane/agent/work?siteId=...&id=...` reads saved local work. Omit `id` for the newest 100 records. Links stay within the installation and require the owner session.
- `POST /api/vane/agent/brief` accepts `siteId` and optional end-exclusive `to`. It analyzes seven ended local days of native observations, excluding imported history. The current brief is owner-requested; automatic weekly production remains unbuilt. It returns up to five supported opportunities, with figures, report links and linked change drafts. With fewer than three supported opportunities it reports the evidence shortfall explicitly. Entry and exit opportunities require at least five entries and exits at least half the entries; a campaign requires at least five visits and conversion above the site's native visit rate; loading opportunities require five measured loads and p75 LCP above 2,500 ms; a funnel opportunity requires five entrants and fewer than half reaching the next step. These are review heuristics, not proof of causal lift. Search opportunities and peer-qualified performance opportunities remain unbuilt.
- `POST /api/vane/agent/dismiss` accepts `siteId` and `type`: entry-exit, campaign, slow-page or funnel. The type is omitted from later briefs and from re-requesting the same brief. It does not delete the historical evidence or drafts.
- `POST /api/vane/agent/measure` accepts `siteId`, `fixId`, `from`, `to`, `comparedFrom`, `comparedTo`, and optional `experiment` and `variant`. It requires a verified instrumentation fix deployed before the measured window. A local draft cannot stand for a shipped change. Ended windows must be non-overlapping, equal in length, match weekdays and span at most 31 days. Experiment intervals and minimum samples come from the ordinary experiment report. Observed coverage or definition changes withhold a positive verdict. Before-and-after work remains inconclusive; complete historical coverage, approved revert and positive-result acceptance proof remain unbuilt.
- `POST /api/vane/agent/pursue` accepts `siteId`, `itemId` and the same evidence windows as the movement investigation. It investigates first, saves the hypotheses and unknowns, and comments an evidence link on the unresolved follow-up. It sends no new message and never automatically closes the item. Existing owner-configured follow-up reminders continue normally. A person resolves through the existing follow-up form. Developer contact, verified recovery and automatic closure with intact coverage remain unbuilt.

Saved drafts, briefs, measurements and pursuits are anonymous aggregate work records, not visitor histories. Whole-installation archives include them. History erasure removes them; retention removes work whose evidence begins before the retained window. Dismissals persist as owner preferences until site deletion. Per-site transfers exclude this work. Derived writes check the original settings version and deletion journal before committing, so an in-flight result cannot recreate erased evidence.

AGENT-003 through AGENT-006 remain partial. Their original acceptance requirements still apply. Future shipping and scheduled work must carry active owner authority, explicit grant scope, revocation, approved material copy and spend, custody and evidence controls before becoming available.
