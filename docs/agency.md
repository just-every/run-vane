# Agency client workspaces

The owner opens **Agency clients** in the app, creates a client, assigns an existing site and sends a client invitation. Native email-link authentication owns the invitation and sign-in. A client user is a Viewer of that client’s current sites. Client membership is resolved on every request; an empty client grants no sites. Moving a site changes access immediately and updates the grants used by alert and follow-up recipients in the same transaction. Scheduled email rechecks access before dispatch, including an owner-created external subscription to a known client user’s address. Remove a user through Team. Agency settings and cross-client management are owner-only and audited.

Save the agency brand, upload its PNG, JPEG or WebP logo (at most 256 KB), and enable the brand to publish it. Signed-in site reports, public reports, shared links and scheduled email inherit it. Enable a client’s brand to use its own name, logo, accent and background instead. Disabling the client brand restores agency inheritance and immediately stops serving that client’s uploaded logo. Logos are served from the installation and custom report domains only while their brand is enabled; the app never fetches a remote logo. Uploaded images are public presentation assets and must contain no private information. SVG and HTML are refused. The homepage, installer identity and native sign-in keep the installation’s existing branding. Existing mail-provider sender identity remains configured through the installation; a custom report domain changes report links, logo URLs and unsubscribe URLs, not the provider’s verified From address.

An owner may configure an HTTPS origin such as `https://reports.agency.example`. Attach that hostname to this Worker and configure its DNS/TLS in the owner’s Cloudflare account first, then attest to control of it in the brand form. Vane does not purchase domains, change DNS, issue certificates or probe remote hosts. An agency hostname serves all deliberately public site reports and bearer shared links. A client hostname serves only that client’s public reports and shared links. Neither serves native sign-in, private app routes, APIs or the tracking script. Only the report styles/fonts, uploaded raster logos and valid unsubscribe links are also served. Shared-link passwords and revocation continue to apply. Disabling a brand or removing its hostname immediately closes those custom-host doors; canonical public/shared links remain available under their existing publication settings.

Whole-installation archives include agency grouping, membership, branding and uploaded images. Site transfers deliberately omit installation-level client identities, member grants and custom-host configuration; assign the imported site to a local client. Deleting visitor history leaves these configuration records alone. Deleting a site removes its client assignment.

The owner’s attestation is a conservative default awaiting ct’s policy ruling: **may an owner attach an already-controlled HTTPS hostname by attestation alone, or must Vane verify DNS/control before serving it?** Client users default to Viewers and owner-only management; ct should confirm those authority defaults. Keep AGENCY-001 partial until these choices and a held-account custom-domain DNS/TLS journey are resolved. Local Worker tests prove routing behavior, not provider domain attachment.

`tests/agency.test.mjs` adds two public-door flows: two-client isolation/branding/email/custom-host behavior and the actual app at 1440/390. Set `VANE_SCREEN_DIR` to capture the populated agency overview, brand settings and a branded client report. The existing TEAM-001 and REPORT-001 flows retain their full authorization and delivery semantics.
